THE ROUTE
1. Rosemary Beach · start
   See & Do: Walk the town green and the boardwalks.
   Tip: Park once, and bike the trail along 30A.
2. Alys Beach · mile 1 · 1 mi / 5 min from Rosemary Beach via 30A
   See: Walk the white streets of town.
   Tip: Come at sunset for the best light.
3. Seaside · mile 7 · 6 mi / 15 min from Alys Beach via 30A
   See & Do: Central Square and the Airstream Row food trucks.
   Tip: Parking fills fast in summer. Bike in.
4. Grayton Beach State Park · mile 9 · 2 mi / 5 min from Seaside via 30A
   Do: Paddle Western Lake, then walk over to the beach.
   Tip: The state park charges a small entry fee per car.
5. Eden Gardens · mile 15 · 6 mi / 15 min from Grayton Beach State Park via 30A and CR 395
   See: The Wesley mansion and its gardens.
   Tip: House tours run on select days. Check ahead.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- Park once and bike the Timpoochee Trail between towns.
- Summer weekends are packed. Spring and fall are calmer.
- Watch the beach flags. Rip currents are common.
- State parks charge a small entry fee per car.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
