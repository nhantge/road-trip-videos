THE ROUTE
1. Springdale (3,900 ft) · start
   See & Do: The Zion Canyon shuttle, and the Riverside Walk.
   Tip: Get to the park early. Parking fills fast.
2. Canyon Overlook (5,150 ft) · mile 7 · 7 mi / 20 min from Springdale via UT 9
   Hike: Canyon Overlook Trail, about a mile round trip.
   Tip: Big RVs and trailers need a tunnel permit.
3. Checkerboard Mesa (5,828 ft) · mile 12 · 5 mi / 10 min from Canyon Overlook via UT 9
   See: The Checkerboard Mesa viewpoint.
   Wildlife: Bighorn sheep on the slickrock.
4. Red Canyon (7,400 ft) · mile 69 · 57 mi / 1 hr 10 min from Checkerboard Mesa via US 89 and UT 12
   See: The two rock tunnels on Highway 12.
   Hike: The short Pink Ledges loop by the visitor center.
5. Bryce Canyon (8,000 ft) · mile 82 · 13 mi / 20 min from Red Canyon via UT 12 and UT 63
   See: Sunset and Sunrise Points over the Bryce Amphitheater.
   Hike: Navajo Loop and Queen's Garden, about 3 miles.
   Tip: It's cold up here. Bring layers, even in summer.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- Zion Canyon is mostly shuttle-only. Get there early for parking.
- RVs and trailers 7'10" wide or 11'4" tall need a $15 tunnel permit.
- Bryce sits around 8,000 feet. Bring layers, even in summer.
- One national park pass covers both Zion and Bryce.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
