THE ROUTE
1. Silver Springs · start
   See & Do: A glass-bottom boat tour, and kayaking the Silver River.
   Tip: Weekday mornings are the calmest.
2. Rainbow Springs · mile 30 · 30 mi / 45 min from Silver Springs via SR 40 and CR 484
   See & Do: The gardens and waterfalls, and kayaking the Rainbow River.
   Swim: Tubing on the river in the warm months.
   Tip: Tubing is seasonal. Check before you go.
3. Crystal River · mile 52 · 22 mi / 35 min from Rainbow Springs via US 41 and US 19
   See: Manatees from the Three Sisters Springs boardwalk.
   Do: A guided manatee tour in Kings Bay.
   Tip: Look, don't touch. Never chase or touch a manatee.
4. Homosassa Springs · mile 60 · 8 mi / 15 min from Crystal River via US 19
   See: The underwater observatory, and the manatee program.
   Tip: Winter mornings are best for manatees.
5. Weeki Wachee · mile 81 · 21 mi / 30 min from Homosassa Springs via US 19
   See: The underwater mermaid show.
   Do: Kayak or paddleboard the Weeki Wachee River.
   Tip: Check show times before you go.

Best season: November to March for manatees

GOOD TO KNOW
- Manatees gather in the springs from November to March.
- Look, don't touch: never chase, touch or feed a manatee.
- Use reef-safe sunscreen in the springs, or none at all.
- Tubing and swimming rules change by season. Check each park.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
