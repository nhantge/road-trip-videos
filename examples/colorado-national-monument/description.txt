THE ROUTE
1. Fruita (4,510 ft) · start
   See & Do: Dinosaur Journey Museum, and downtown Fruita.
   Tip: Fill up and grab snacks here. There's nothing on Rim Rock Drive.
2. Saddlehorn (5,702 ft) · mile 6 · 6 mi / 15 min from Fruita via Rim Rock Drive
   See: Canyon Rim Trail, an easy walk along the edge.
   Do: Talk to a ranger, and fill your water bottles.
   Tip: Open daily except Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day.
3. Independence Monument View (5,542 ft) · mile 8 · 2 mi / 5 min from Saddlehorn via Rim Rock Drive
   See: Independence Monument from the Grand View overlook.
   Hike: The Monument Canyon Trail goes right past its base.
   Tip: Sunset turns these cliffs bright orange.
4. Cold Shivers Point (5,966 ft) · mile 20 · 12 mi / 25 min from Independence Monument View via Rim Rock Drive
   See: Columbus Canyon, straight down from the railing.
   Tip: Hold onto little ones. The rim is a long way down.
5. Grand Junction (4,591 ft) · mile 29 · 9 mi / 20 min from Cold Shivers Point via Rim Rock Drive
   See & Do: Main Street's sculptures, and wineries in nearby Palisade.
   Tip: Peach season peaks in August.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- There's an entrance fee, or use an America the Beautiful pass.
- Turn your headlights on in the three tunnels, and share the road with cyclists.
- Bring water. There's no food or gas on Rim Rock Drive.
- Summer afternoons are hot. Drive early, or come in spring and fall.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
