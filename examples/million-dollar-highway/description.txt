THE ROUTE
1. Ouray (7,792 ft) · start
   See & Do: Box Cañon Falls, and the Ouray Hot Springs Pool.
   Hike: The Ouray Perimeter Trail loops all the way around town.
   Tip: Start early. Morning light in the gorge is spectacular.
2. Bear Creek Falls (8,378 ft) · mile 2 · 2 mi / 8 min from Ouray via US 550
   See: The falls from the overlook platform right off the highway.
   Hike: Bear Creek Trail climbs from here, with big views fast.
   Tip: The lot is small. Get there early on summer weekends.
3. Red Mountain Pass (11,018 ft) · mile 13 · 10 mi / 25 min from Bear Creek Falls via US 550
   On the way, worth a stop: Ironton ghost town. Walk among the cabins of an 1880s mining camp, a short stroll off the highway.
   On the way, worth a stop: Yankee Girl Mine. Its wooden headframe stands right beside the road. One of the richest silver mines of the 1880s.
   See: The red peaks of Red Mountain and old mines on every slope.
   Do: Walk the summit area. Wildflowers peak in July.
   Tip: It can snow here in any month. Bring a jacket.
4. Silverton (9,318 ft) · mile 24 · 11 mi / 20 min from Red Mountain Pass via US 550
   On the way, worth a stop: Ice Lake Basin. Bright blue alpine lakes. The trail starts up South Mineral Creek Road; plan a full day.
   See & Do: Ride the steam train, or tour the Old Hundred Gold Mine.
   Eat: Handlebars Food & Saloon, right on Greene Street.
   Stay: The Grand Imperial Hotel, built in 1883.

Best season: June to early October

GOOD TO KNOW
- There's no gas between Ouray and Silverton. Fill up before you go.
- Use the pullouts for photos and let faster traffic by. Never stop in a curve.
- Snow can close Red Mountain Pass outside summer. Check COtrip.org first.
- Give it a full day: the drive is an hour, the stops are the fun part.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
