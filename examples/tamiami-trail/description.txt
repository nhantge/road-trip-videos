THE ROUTE
1. Little Havana · start
   See & Do: Domino Park and the Calle Ocho Walk of Fame.
   Eat: A cafecito and a Cuban sandwich.
   Tip: Fill up here. Gas is sparse in the Everglades.
2. Shark Valley · mile 33 · 33 mi / 45 min from Little Havana via US 41
   See & Do: Ride the tram, or bike the 15-mile loop to the observation tower.
   Wildlife: Alligators, turtles and wading birds, right by the path.
   Tip: Book tram seats ahead in winter. Waits can be long.
3. Big Cypress · mile 52 · 19 mi / 25 min from Shark Valley via US 41
   See: Alligators along the canal at the Oasis Visitor Center.
   Do: Walk the boardwalk at Kirby Storter Roadside Park.
   Tip: Stay behind the rails, and never feed the gators.
4. Everglades City · mile 75 · 23 mi / 30 min from Big Cypress via US 41 and SR 29
   On the way, worth a stop: Ochopee Post Office. The smallest post office in the United States, about 61 square feet. Mail a postcard!
   Do: A boat tour of the Ten Thousand Islands.
   Eat: Fresh stone crab claws, in season from mid-October.
   Tip: Bring bug spray, especially at dusk.
5. Naples · mile 111 · 36 mi / 50 min from Everglades City via SR 29 and US 41
   See & Do: The Naples Pier and Fifth Avenue South.
   Tip: Get to the pier early for sunset parking.

Best season: November to April, the dry season

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fill up in Miami. Gas is sparse across the Everglades.
- Winter is the dry season: fewer bugs and more wildlife.
- Bring bug spray, especially around dusk.
- Never feed or approach alligators.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
