THE ROUTE
1. Denver (5,280 ft) · start
   See & Do: Union Station and the Capitol's mile-high step.
   Tip: Gas and snacks here. The first real town is an hour out.
2. Kenosha Pass (10,001 ft) · mile 63 · 63 mi / 1 hr 15 min from Denver via US 285
   See: The view across South Park, a 50-mile-wide mountain basin.
   Do: Walk a stretch of the Colorado Trail, which crosses at the summit.
   Tip: Aspens peak in the last two weeks of September.
3. Buena Vista (7,965 ft) · mile 119 · 56 mi / 1 hr from Kenosha Pass via US 285
   See & Do: Raft Browns Canyon on the Arkansas. Soak at Mount Princeton Hot Springs.
   Eat: Eddyline Restaurant & Brewery, on the river.
   Tip: Book rafting a few days ahead in July and August.
4. Monarch Pass (11,312 ft) · mile 160 · 41 mi / 50 min from Buena Vista via US 50
   See: The Continental Divide marker and 360-degree views of the Sawatch Range.
   Do: Ride the Monarch Crest tram to 12,000 ft (summer only).
   Tip: Weather changes fast up here. Bring a jacket even in July.
5. Blue Mesa Reservoir (7,519 ft) · mile 219 · 59 mi / 1 hr 5 min from Monarch Pass via US 50
   See: The Dillon Pinnacles, volcanic spires on the north shore.
   Do: Stop at the Elk Creek Visitor Center, or rent a boat at the marina.
   Tip: Wind picks up most afternoons. Paddle in the morning.
6. Ouray (7,792 ft) · mile 305 · 86 mi / 1 hr 40 min from Blue Mesa Reservoir via US 50 · US 550
   See & Do: Ouray Hot Springs Pool. Walk into Box Cañon Falls.
   Eat: Ouray Brewery, with a rooftop deck on Main Street.
   Stay: The Beaumont Hotel, built in 1886.

Best season: Late June to early October

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fill up in Buena Vista, Salida, Gunnison and Montrose. Gaps between pumps get long.
- Monarch Pass can see snow in any month. Check COtrip.org before you go.
- Leave Denver early on Fridays. US 285 backs up with weekend traffic.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
