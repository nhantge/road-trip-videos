## After the upload finishes

1. **Subtitles:** Subtitles > Add language > English > Upload file > With timing > `captions.srt`.
2. **End screen:** Editor > End screen > add Subscribe and one video element (your latest
   upload, or "Best for viewer") over the last 7 seconds. The end card leaves room for
   them on the left (Subscribe) and right (Up next).
3. **Chapters:** check they appear on the progress bar. YouTube needs the first at 0:00,
   at least three, and each 10 seconds or longer; `description.txt` already meets that.
4. **Pinned comment (optional):** ask viewers where to drive next, for example
   *"Which Colorado drive should we map next? Million Dollar Highway is up next."*

## Before you publish

- Watch the first 30 seconds with sound on: sting, hook, then the stats card.
