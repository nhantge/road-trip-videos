THE ROUTE
1. Denver (5,280 ft) · start
   Tip: Leave early. I-70 traffic builds fast on weekends.
2. Georgetown (8,530 ft) · mile 45 · 45 mi / 55 min from Denver via I-70
   See & Do: Victorian main street, and the Georgetown Loop Railroad.
   Tip: Watch for bighorn sheep on the cliffs by Georgetown Lake.
3. Frisco (9,106 ft) · mile 72 · 27 mi / 35 min from Georgetown via I-70
   On the way, worth a stop: Dillon Reservoir. A huge mountain lake with a paved bike path all the way around.
   See & Do: Main Street, and the marina on Dillon Reservoir.
   Tip: Ski areas all around: Breckenridge, Keystone and Copper Mountain.
4. Vail (8,150 ft) · mile 97 · 25 mi / 30 min from Frisco via I-70
   See & Do: Vail Village, Lionshead, and the Gore Creek path.
   Tip: Park in the town parking structures.
5. Glenwood Canyon (5,977 ft) · mile 151 · 54 mi / 55 min from Vail via I-70
   On the way, worth a stop: Hanging Lake. A turquoise lake below a waterfall, up a steep 1.2-mile trail. Permit required.
   See: The canyon walls and the Colorado River.
   Do: Walk or bike the Glenwood Canyon Recreation Path.
   Tip: Westbound, there's no Hanging Lake exit. Use Grizzly Creek and double back.
6. Glenwood Springs (5,761 ft) · mile 157 · 6 mi / 10 min from Glenwood Canyon via I-70
   See & Do: Glenwood Hot Springs Pool, and the Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park.
   History: Doc Holliday's grave is in Linwood Cemetery, a short hike up from town.
   Tip: Hot springs are best on a cold winter evening.

Best season: Year-round; fall and winter for the hot springs

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fall to spring, Colorado's traction law can apply on I-70. Check COtrip.org first.
- Avoid eastbound I-70 on Sunday afternoons, when ski traffic backs up for hours.
- Hanging Lake needs a permit year-round. Book ahead.
- Glenwood Canyon can close for rockfall. Check before you go.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
