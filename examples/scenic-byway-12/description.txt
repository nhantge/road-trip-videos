THE ROUTE
1. Bryce Canyon (8,000 ft) · start
   See: Sunrise Point before you hit the road.
   Tip: Start early. There's a lot to see today.
2. Escalante (5,820 ft) · mile 47 · 47 mi / 1 hr from Bryce Canyon via UT 12
   See & Do: Escalante Petrified Forest State Park.
   Tip: Fill up here. Services are few until Boulder.
3. Lower Calf Creek Falls (5,300 ft) · mile 62 · 15 mi / 20 min from Escalante via UT 12
   Hike: About 6 miles round trip, mostly sandy.
   Tip: Start early in summer, and bring plenty of water.
4. Boulder (6,700 ft) · mile 74 · 12 mi / 20 min from Lower Calf Creek Falls via UT 12
   See: Anasazi State Park Museum.
   Eat: Hell's Backbone Grill, a local favorite.
   Tip: The Hogback has no shoulders. Take it slow.
5. Torrey (6,840 ft) · mile 111 · 37 mi / 50 min from Boulder via UT 12
   Eat: Torrey's cafes and food trucks.
   Tip: Capitol Reef is just 11 miles east.

Best season: May to October

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fill up in Escalante. Services are few until Boulder and Torrey.
- The Hogback has steep drop-offs and no shoulders. Take it slow.
- Boulder Mountain can see snow from fall into spring.
- Bring plenty of water for the Calf Creek hike.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
