THE ROUTE
1. Leadville (10,152 ft) · start
   See & Do: Harrison Avenue's Victorian main street and the Tabor Opera House.
   Eat: Grab breakfast on Harrison Avenue before you head out.
   Tip: Fill up here. Services are few until Aspen.
2. Twin Lakes (9,200 ft) · mile 20 · 20 mi / 30 min from Leadville via US 24
   On the way, worth a stop: Mount Elbert. Colorado's highest peak, over 14,400 feet. Its summit trail is a classic first fourteener.
   See: The lakes, with Mount Elbert rising right behind them.
   Do: Paddle, fish or picnic on the shore.
   Tip: This is your last easy stop before the big climb.
3. Independence Pass (12,095 ft) · mile 38 · 18 mi / 35 min from Twin Lakes via CO 82
   See: The Continental Divide sign and views across the Sawatch Range.
   Do: Walk the short paved trail to the overlook.
   Tip: It's cold and windy up here, even in July. Bring a jacket.
4. Independence Ghost Town (10,830 ft) · mile 41 · 3 mi / 10 min from Independence Pass via CO 82
   See: Weathered log cabins from the 1880s.
   History: The gold ran out within a few years, and the miners moved on to Aspen's silver.
   Tip: It's a protected historic site. Look, but don't touch.
5. Aspen (7,908 ft) · mile 57 · 16 mi / 35 min from Independence Ghost Town via CO 82
   On the way, worth a stop: The Grottos. Smooth granite, waterfalls and ice caves along the Roaring Fork, an easy walk from the road.
   See: The Maroon Bells, Colorado's most photographed peaks. Reservations required.
   Do: Ride the Silver Queen Gondola up Aspen Mountain in summer.
   Tip: Downtown parking is tight. Use the free town buses.

Best season: Late June to September

GOOD TO KNOW
- The pass closes in winter, usually November to late May. Check COtrip.org first.
- The road narrows to about one lane in spots. Go slow, and long RVs should check CDOT limits.
- Fill up in Leadville. Services are few until Aspen.
- Storms build fast up top on summer afternoons. Cross the pass in the morning.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
