THE ROUTE
1. Moab (4,025 ft) · start
   See & Do: Jeep tours, mountain biking and rafting on the Colorado.
   Tip: Book jeep and rafting trips ahead in spring and fall.
2. Arches National Park (5,150 ft) · mile 14 · 14 mi / 30 min from Moab via US 191 and Arches Scenic Drive
   See: The Windows, Balanced Rock and Park Avenue.
   Hike: Delicate Arch, about 3 miles round trip.
   Tip: Get there early. Parking lots fill by mid-morning.
3. Dead Horse Point (5,900 ft) · mile 47 · 33 mi / 50 min from Arches National Park via US 191 and UT 313
   See: The Dead Horse Point overlook, best at sunset.
   Tip: State park fee. National park passes don't count here.
4. Grand View Point (6,080 ft) · mile 66 · 19 mi / 30 min from Dead Horse Point via Grand View Point Road
   On the way, worth a stop: Mesa Arch. A short walk to an arch right on the canyon rim. Famous at sunrise.
   See: Grand View Point, and Mesa Arch on the way.
   Tip: No food or water in the park. Pack it in.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- Arches doesn't need a timed-entry reservation in 2026, but lots fill early.
- Dead Horse Point is a state park, so national park passes don't cover it.
- There's no food or water in Island in the Sky. Pack it in.
- Summer days top 100 degrees. Hike early.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
