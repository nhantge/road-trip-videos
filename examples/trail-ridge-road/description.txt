Best season: Late June to September

GOOD TO KNOW
- The road is usually open late May to mid-October. Check the park's road status first.
- In peak season the park needs timed entry reservations. Book ahead on Recreation.gov.
- There's no gas in the park. Fill up in Estes Park or Grand Lake.
- It's often 15 to 20 degrees colder up top. Bring layers, and go early to beat the storms.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
