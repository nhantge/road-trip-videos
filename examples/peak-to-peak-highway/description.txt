THE ROUTE
1. Estes Park (7,522 ft) · start
   See & Do: Elkhorn Avenue, and elk grazing right in town in the fall.
   Tip: Fill up here. Gas gets sparse until Nederland.
2. Chapel on the Rock (8,460 ft) · mile 9 · 9 mi / 15 min from Estes Park via CO 7
   On the way, worth a stop: Lily Lake. A flat, easy loop around a lake with a big view of Longs Peak.
   See: The chapel and Mount Meeker right behind it.
   Tip: The grounds are open to visitors. Please be quiet and respectful.
3. Ward (9,450 ft) · mile 30 · 21 mi / 35 min from Chapel on the Rock via CO 7 and CO 72
   On the way, worth a stop: Brainard Lake. An alpine lake under the Indian Peaks. Timed-entry reservations in summer.
   See: Old miners' cabins and mine ruins on the hillsides.
   Hike: Brainard Lake and the Indian Peaks are just up the road.
   Tip: Ward is tiny and residential. Look from the road.
4. Nederland (8,228 ft) · mile 42 · 12 mi / 20 min from Ward via CO 72
   See & Do: The Carousel of Happiness and Barker Reservoir.
   Eat: Grab lunch in town. It's the biggest stop on the byway.
   Tip: Fill up here before the drive down to Black Hawk.
5. Black Hawk (8,042 ft) · mile 55 · 13 mi / 25 min from Nederland via CO 119
   See & Do: Central City's Victorian main street and its 1878 opera house.
   Tip: Casinos are 21 and up. Parking garages are usually free.

Best season: Mid to late September for aspens

GOOD TO KNOW
- Aspens peak in mid to late September. Go on a weekday to beat the crowds.
- The road is open year-round, but it's snowy and icy in winter. Check COtrip.org.
- Brainard Lake needs a timed-entry reservation in summer. Book on Recreation.gov.
- Casinos in Black Hawk and Central City are 21 and up.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
