THE ROUTE
1. Silverton (9,318 ft) · start
   See & Do: Greene Street's historic storefronts, and the steam train arriving midday.
   Eat: Handlebars Food & Saloon.
   Tip: Fill up here. Services are thin until Purgatory.
2. Molas Pass (10,910 ft) · mile 6 · 6 mi / 15 min from Silverton via US 550
   On the way, worth a stop: Molas Lake. A small mountain lake with a campground and huge views. Great for a picnic or a paddle.
   See: The overlook across to the Grenadier Range and the Animas River gorge.
   Do: Hike a stretch of the Colorado Trail, which crosses right here.
   Tip: Afternoon storms roll in fast in summer. Come in the morning.
3. Coal Bank Pass (10,640 ft) · mile 13 · 7 mi / 15 min from Molas Pass via US 550
   On the way, worth a stop: Andrews Lake. A quiet lake just south of Molas Pass, with an easy loop trail and great reflections.
   See: Engineer Mountain, towering right over the pass.
   Hike: The Pass Creek Trail heads toward Engineer Mountain from here.
   Tip: The descent south is long and steep. Use lower gears.
4. Durango (6,512 ft) · mile 48 · 35 mi / 50 min from Coal Bank Pass via US 550
   On the way, worth a stop: Purgatory Resort. In summer: an alpine coaster, scenic chairlift rides and mountain biking.
   On the way, worth a stop: Trimble Hot Springs. Natural hot springs pools just north of Durango. Perfect after a day on the road.
   See & Do: Ride the Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad. Walk Main Avenue.
   Eat: Steamworks Brewing Co.
   Stay: The Strater Hotel, open since 1887.

Best season: June to early October

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fill up in Silverton. There's little in the way of services until Purgatory.
- Pull over at the Molas Pass overlook on the east side of the road. Don't skip it!
- Watch for deer and elk south of Purgatory, especially at dawn and dusk.
- Make it a loop: drive one way and ride the steam train the other (book ahead).

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
