THE ROUTE
1. Alamosa (7,544 ft) · start
   See & Do: Rent sandboards and sleds in town.
   Wildlife: Sandhill cranes stop over near Monte Vista every spring and fall.
   Tip: Fill up and stock up here.
2. Zapata Falls (9,000 ft) · mile 30 · 30 mi / 40 min from Alamosa via US 160 and CO 150
   Hike: About half a mile each way from the parking area.
   Tip: Wear shoes that can get wet, and watch for slippery ice in winter.
3. Visitor Center (8,175 ft) · mile 37 · 7 mi / 10 min from Zapata Falls via CO 150
   See: The dunefield view from the visitor center.
   Do: Pick up a map and check the sand temperature forecast.
   Tip: There's an entrance fee, or use an America the Beautiful pass.
4. Great Sand Dunes (8,112 ft) · mile 38 · 1 mi / 5 min from Visitor Center via Park road
   Do: Climb High Dune, then sandboard back down.
   See: Medano Creek in late spring, and the stars after dark.
   Tip: The sand gets dangerously hot on summer afternoons.

Best season: Late spring for Medano Creek; fall for cool sand

GOOD TO KNOW
- In summer the sand can hit 150 degrees. Climb early or late in the day.
- Rent a sandboard or sand sled in Alamosa or Mosca before you arrive.
- Medano Creek flows from late April into June, and peaks in late May.
- It's an International Dark Sky Park. Stay after sunset for the stars.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
