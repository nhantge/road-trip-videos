THE ROUTE
1. Monument Valley (5,567 ft) · start
   Do: The 17-mile Valley Drive, or a Navajo-guided tour.
   Tip: Federal park passes aren't accepted here.
2. Forrest Gump Point (5,254 ft) · mile 17 · 17 mi / 25 min from Monument Valley via US 163
   See: The view south down US 163 to Monument Valley.
   Tip: Pull fully off the road. Don't stand in the lanes.
3. Mexican Hat (4,240 ft) · mile 27 · 10 mi / 15 min from Forrest Gump Point via US 163
   See: Mexican Hat Rock, just north of town.
   Tip: Gas and a few motels. Plan ahead.
4. Goosenecks State Park (4,950 ft) · mile 36 · 9 mi / 15 min from Mexican Hat via UT 261 and UT 316
   See: The overlook, right by the parking lot.
   Tip: There's no water here. Bring your own.
5. Moki Dugway (6,350 ft) · mile 48 · 12 mi / 30 min from Goosenecks State Park via UT 261
   On the way, worth a stop: Valley of the Gods. A 17-mile dirt road through giant red buttes. Best with high clearance after rain.
   See: The view back over the Valley of the Gods.
   Tip: Unpaved, steep and narrow. Not for RVs or trailers.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- Monument Valley is a Navajo Tribal Park: $8 a person, and federal passes aren't accepted.
- Navajo guides are required for most of the valley beyond the Valley Drive.
- The Moki Dugway is unpaved, steep and narrow. Not for RVs or trailers.
- Gas up in Mexican Hat or Bluff. Services are few.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
