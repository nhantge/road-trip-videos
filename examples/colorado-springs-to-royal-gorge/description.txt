THE ROUTE
1. Garden of the Gods (6,400 ft) · start
   See: The Central Garden loop and Balanced Rock.
   Do: Walk the easy paved Perkins Central Garden Trail.
   Tip: Parking fills by mid-morning. Come early.
2. Manitou Springs (6,412 ft) · mile 4 · 4 mi / 10 min from Garden of the Gods via US 24
   See & Do: The mineral springs, and the Pikes Peak Cog Railway.
   Hike: The Manitou Incline, if you're feeling brave.
   Tip: Book the cog railway ahead. It sells out.
3. Cañon City (5,332 ft) · mile 54 · 50 mi / 1 hr from Manitou Springs via CO 115 and US 50
   See & Do: Skyline Drive, and the Royal Gorge Route Railroad depot.
   Tip: Skyline Drive is one-way and narrow. No trailers.
4. Royal Gorge Bridge (6,500 ft) · mile 66 · 12 mi / 20 min from Cañon City via US 50
   See & Do: Walk the bridge, ride the gondola, or try the zipline.
   Tip: It's a ticketed park. Buy online to save time.

Best season: Year-round; fall for cool, clear days

GOOD TO KNOW
- Garden of the Gods is free. Go early for parking and golden light.
- The Royal Gorge Bridge is inside a ticketed park. Buy tickets online.
- Skyline Drive is one-way and narrow. Leave the trailer at home.
- Ride the Royal Gorge Route train from Cañon City for the view from below.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
