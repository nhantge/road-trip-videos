THE ROUTE
1. Torrey (6,840 ft) · start
   See: The stars, if you stay the night.
   Tip: Fill up here. Gas is limited until Hanksville.
2. Capitol Reef (5,400 ft) · mile 11 · 11 mi / 15 min from Torrey via UT 24
   See: The Petroglyph Panel and the Fruita orchards.
   Eat: Fresh pie at the Gifford Homestead.
   Hike: Hickman Bridge, about 2 miles round trip.
3. Hanksville (4,300 ft) · mile 48 · 37 mi / 45 min from Capitol Reef via UT 24
   On the way, worth a stop: Factory Butte. A lone butte rising out of the gray badlands. Pull off for the view.
   Eat: Stan's Burger Shak.
   Tip: Fuel up. Services are few out here.
4. Goblin Valley (4,962 ft) · mile 80 · 32 mi / 40 min from Hanksville via UT 24 and Goblin Valley Road
   Do: Wander the Valley of Goblins. There are no set trails.
   Tip: Bring water and sun protection. There's no shade.

Best season: Spring and fall

GOOD TO KNOW
- Fill up in Torrey or Hanksville. There's little in between.
- Gifford Homestead pies often sell out. Come early.
- Goblin Valley has almost no shade. Bring water and sun protection.
- Torrey has some of the darkest night skies around. Stay for the stars.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
