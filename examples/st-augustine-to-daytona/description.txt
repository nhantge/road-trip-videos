THE ROUTE
1. St. Augustine · start
   See & Do: Castillo de San Marcos and St. George Street.
   Tip: Use the Historic Downtown Parking Facility, then walk.
2. Fort Matanzas · mile 15 · 15 mi / 25 min from St. Augustine via A1A
   Do: The free ferry out to the fort.
   Tip: Ferry tickets are free but limited. Get there early.
3. Marineland · mile 19 · 4 mi / 10 min from Fort Matanzas via A1A
   See & Do: Dolphin encounters at Marineland.
   Tip: Book dolphin programs ahead.
4. Flagler Beach · mile 33 · 14 mi / 25 min from Marineland via A1A
   See & Do: Walk the Flagler Beach Pier.
   Tip: Grab lunch at a beachside spot right on A1A.
5. Daytona Beach · mile 60 · 27 mi / 45 min from Flagler Beach via A1A
   Do: Drive on the beach, then visit Daytona International Speedway.
   Tip: Beach driving has a fee and set hours. Check before you go.

Best season: Fall and spring

GOOD TO KNOW
- Park once in St. Augustine and walk the old town.
- Fort Matanzas ferry tickets are free but limited. Go early.
- Beach driving in Daytona has a fee and set hours. Check before you go.
- A1A runs right along the dunes. Watch for bikes and walkers.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
