THE ROUTE
1. Key Largo · start
   See & Do: Snorkel or ride a glass-bottom boat at John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park.
   Tip: Book reef trips early. Calm mornings are best.
2. Islamorada · mile 20 · 20 mi / 25 min from Key Largo via US 1
   See & Do: Feed the tarpon at Robbie's Marina.
   Eat: Fresh-caught fish, right on the water.
   Tip: Fishing charters leave early. Book the day before.
3. Marathon · mile 52 · 32 mi / 40 min from Islamorada via US 1
   See & Do: Tour the Turtle Hospital, then relax at Sombrero Beach.
   Tip: Turtle Hospital tours are guided. Reserve ahead.
4. Bahia Honda State Park · mile 65 · 13 mi / 20 min from Marathon via US 1
   See & Do: Swim at Sandspur Beach, and climb up to the old bridge overlook.
   Tip: The park fills up by mid-morning in winter. Come early.
5. Key West · mile 102 · 37 mi / 50 min from Bahia Honda State Park via US 1
   See & Do: The Hemingway Home and its six-toed cats, and Duval Street.
   Eat: Key lime pie, of course.
   Tip: Park once, then walk or bike. Parking is tight.

Best season: December to April

GOOD TO KNOW
- Addresses use mile markers, counting down to Mile Marker 0 in Key West.
- Winter is peak season. Book Key West hotels early.
- Hurricane season runs June through November. Watch the forecast.
- Reef trips at Pennekamp fill up on weekends. Book ahead.

Mileage and drive times are approximate and don't include stops. Check road conditions before you go.
